Return and refund policy

1. What is the return policy?

Returns, exchanges and replacements are accepted only for a manufacturing defect, damage in transit, or an incorrect shipment. Clear photographs of the issue must be shared within 48 hours of receipt, by email to helpdesk@companystore.io.

2. When is a refund paid?

Refunds on eligible returns are processed within 7 to 10 business days of the item reaching our warehouse, and credited against the original payment method. Shipping charges on the original order are not refunded.

3. How do I return an item?

Raise the return within 10 days of delivery, repack the item in its original packaging with the return documentation included, and wait for our logistics team to coordinate pickup.

4. How do I track a return?

Our support team emails the pickup tracking details. A return is tracked the same way as a delivery.

5. My item arrived damaged or defective.

Contact customer service immediately with photographs. A representative decides between return, replacement and refund, depending on what is in stock.

6. An item is missing, or the wrong item arrived.

Contact customer service with the order id and what is wrong. Missing items are shipped separately. An incorrect item is verified on return before a replacement or refund is released.

7. Can I cancel an order?

An order cannot be cancelled once it has been placed and paid for, unless the item cannot be supplied. In that case the full amount is refunded to the original payment method.

Undelivered shipments

If delivery cannot be completed after three attempts, the shipment returns to our warehouse and the items go back into programme inventory.

Lost shipments

If a shipment is lost in transit, our helpdesk contacts you to process a refund or arrange a reshipment, whichever you prefer.

Questions: helpdesk@companystore.io
