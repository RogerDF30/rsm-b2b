RSM Store, India: FAQ

1. What is the RSM Store?

The RSM Store is an e-commerce platform for RSM India professionals accessing branded merchandise. It serves two purposes:

  • Personal purchases, open to all employees.
  • Business gifting, through the RSM Business Catalog, for select employees only.

2. Who can access the RSM Store?

  • All RSM employees can access the RSM Store to purchase merchandise for personal use at their own expense.
  • Select LOB employees have additional access to the RSM Business Catalog.

3. Are personal purchases reimbursable by the firm?

No. Any purchase made through the RSM Store for personal use is not reimbursable and is paid directly by the employee.

4. What is the RSM Business Catalog?

A curated merchandise catalog available only to Business Account users. It carries items for official firm use and is billed to the LOB's approved gifting budget once the order is approved.

5. I have been given Business Account access. What does that mean?

  • You place orders through the RSM Business Catalog on behalf of your LOB.
  • You keep those orders aligned with your LOB's gifting budget for firm initiatives.

6. How do I sign in?

Business Account users receive credentials by email. Use the email address the account was created against, and the password issued with it. Forgot password sends a reset link to that address.

7. What is the approval process for Business Catalog orders?

An order is raised against your LOB partner's approval, and the partner's approval email is attached to the order as evidence. The partner then approves or rejects from the email the store sends them. Nothing is produced until that decision is recorded.

8. Can anyone else on my team place orders?

No. Only designated Business Account users can access the Business Catalog and place orders.

9. What if I hit a technical problem?

Write to helpdesk@companystore.io with the order id, or raise a ticket with ESS-Enterprise Support Services for anything internal to RSM.

10. How are items delivered?

  • Personal orders are delivered to the employee's address.
  • Business orders are sent to RSM USI office locations, which are listed in the delivery picker at checkout.

11. Is there a return or exchange policy?

Returns and exchanges are limited to damaged or incorrect items. Gifting programme items are not eligible for exchange. The return and refund policy has the detail.
