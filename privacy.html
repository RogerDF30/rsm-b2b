Webstore privacy policy

Hourglass Essentials Pvt Ltd · effective 15 April 2025

Hourglass Essentials Pvt Ltd is the service provider for the company whose branded merchandise is sold on this webstore. This notice describes how we handle personal information in connection with your use of the webstore. By using it you consent to these terms. "Personal information" means information that identifies you as an individual.

Information we collect, and why

To provide the service we may ask you for personal information, including your name, phone number and postal address, which is used to contact or identify you. We may collect:

  • Name
  • Contact information, including email address
  • Demographic information such as postcode, preferences and interests
  • Other information relevant to customer surveys and offers

We use it for internal record keeping, to improve our products and services, to contact you about orders and, where you have agreed to it, for market research and offers.

Payment information

  • All payment card information is processed and stored by our third-party payment gateway providers, in compliance with PCI DSS.
  • Hourglass Essentials never stores complete card numbers or CVV codes.
  • We retain transaction identifiers, masked card information (last four digits), card type, amounts and approval status.
  • Payment information is transmitted using secure encryption protocols.
  • Access to transaction records is limited to staff who need it for order processing, refunds and troubleshooting.
  • Payment processors are contractually required to keep your payment information confidential and secure.

Your payment information is used only to process transactions you authorise. We do not sell, rent or otherwise disclose it, except as necessary to complete your transaction or as required by law.

Log data

When you visit the webstore we collect what your browser sends us: IP address, browser version, the pages you visit, the date and time, time spent on those pages and similar statistics.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser and used as anonymous identifiers. We use them to see which pages are used, so we can improve the store. You can accept or decline cookies in your browser settings; declining them may stop parts of the store from working. We use the data for statistical analysis only.

Service providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate the service, to provide it on our behalf, to perform service-related tasks, or to help us analyse how the service is used. They have access to your personal information only to perform those tasks, and are obliged not to disclose or use it for anything else.

Information we share

We do not sell your personal information. We share it outside Hourglass Essentials Pvt Ltd only:

  • with your consent;
  • for external processing by affiliates or trusted providers, on our instructions and under this policy;
  • with our legal and professional advisors, where their advice is needed;
  • for legal reasons, where we believe in good faith that disclosure is reasonably necessary to meet a law or enforceable request, to enforce terms of service, to detect or prevent fraud and security or technical issues, or to protect against harm to the rights, property or safety of Hourglass Essentials Pvt Ltd, our users or the public.

Security

We use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, with physical, electronic and managerial safeguards against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure and destruction, and we restrict access to staff and contractors who need it and are bound by confidentiality obligations. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your own username and password.

Controlling your information

You can restrict how your personal information is used. Where a form asks whether your details may be used for direct marketing, say no, and if you agreed previously you can change your mind at any time by writing to us. We will not sell, distribute or lease your personal information to third parties without your permission, unless required by law.

You may request details of the personal information we hold about you under applicable data protection law; a small fee may apply. If anything we hold is incorrect or incomplete, write to us and we will correct it promptly.

Links to other sites

The store may link to sites we do not operate. We have no control over their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

Children's privacy

The service is not directed at anyone under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we discover we have, we delete it. Parents and guardians who believe a child has given us information should contact us.

When this policy applies

This policy applies to the services and businesses operated by Hourglass Essentials Pvt Ltd, except those with their own separate privacy policy, and it does not cover the practices of other companies or individuals.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page and take effect immediately, so please review it periodically.
